Privacy Policy

PageWorks · Updated 4 October 2026

PageWorks scans your documents, cleans them up and reads their text entirely on your device. Your scans never leave your iPhone or iPad unless you share or export them yourself. No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.

What the app accesses

  • Camera. Used only while the scan screen is open, to capture documents, ID cards, book pages and QR codes. Photos are processed on the device and stored in the app’s private storage.
  • Photos. Picking photos uses the system picker, which gives the app only the items you select. The app asks for permission only to add to your photo library, when you choose to save pages there.
  • Files you import. PDFs and images you import from the Files app or share from another app are copied into the app’s private storage on your device.
  • Face ID / Touch ID. If you turn on App Lock, the system checks your face, fingerprint or passcode. The app only learns whether the check succeeded; it never receives biometric data.
  • iCloud Drive (optional). If you turn on iCloud backup, a PDF copy of each document is stored in your iCloud Drive by Apple. We have no access to it.
  • Text recognition, translation and summaries. These use Apple’s on-device frameworks (Vision, Translation and Apple Intelligence). The first time you translate into a new language, iOS may download the language from Apple.
  • System search (optional). Document titles, recognized text and tags are added to the search index on your device, so you can find them by swiping down on the Home Screen. The index stays on the device. You can turn this off in Settings; nothing is added while App Lock is on.
  • Motion sensors. Used only while the camera is open, for the level that shows when the phone is held flat over a page. Nothing is recorded.
  • Widgets and the Control Center button. They only open the camera and never read your documents.

What we do not do

  • No data is collected, transmitted to us or stored on our servers. The app has no servers.
  • No third-party analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • No account, email or password is required.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Purchases

PageWorks is sold through the App Store. Apple handles any payment; we never see your card, Apple ID or billing details.

Deleting your data

Deleted documents stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days, where you can recover them or delete them immediately. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the device, including saved signatures. PDF backups in iCloud Drive stay there until you delete them.

Children

PageWorks is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change.

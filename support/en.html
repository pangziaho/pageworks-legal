Common questions

How do I get the best scans?

Use even light and put the page on a background that contrasts with the paper. When the frame turns steady, PageWorks captures automatically; tap Auto at the top to switch to manual capture. Tap the preview to focus.

The edges are wrong. Can I fix them?

Yes. In the review screen tap Crop, or open a page later and tap Crop. Drag the corners or edges (a magnifier helps), or tap Auto to detect again or Full Image to keep the whole photo.

How do I copy both sides of an ID card onto one page?

Choose ID Card mode. Capture the front, turn the card over and capture the back, or tap Front Only. Both sides are placed on one A4 page at actual size, so a 100% print matches the original. Use Cover in Sign to hide numbers you don’t want on the copy, and Watermark to add a purpose such as “For ____ use only”.

Which languages can text recognition read?

More than 25, including English, Japanese, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, and Korean. Pages that mix Japanese and Chinese are read with both models and combined line by line. Choose a language in Settings › Recognition Language, or use Text › More › Recognize Again for an existing document.

How do I make a searchable or password-protected PDF?

Tap Export in a document. Searchable Text is on by default and adds an invisible text layer. Turn on Password Protection to require a password to open the PDF. You can also choose the paper size (including JIS B5/B4), quality, margins and page numbers.

How do I sign a document?

Open a page and tap Sign. Tap Signature to draw a new one or reuse a saved one, then drag and resize it. You can also add text, the date, a black or white cover, or draw with your finger or Apple Pencil.

Can I export to Word or Excel?

Export › Word (DOCX) writes the recognized text into a Word document. On iOS 26 and later, Text › More › Export Tables (CSV) finds tables on the pages and saves them as CSV files that Excel and Numbers can open.

Where are my documents stored? How do I back them up?

Documents are stored only on your device and included in your iPhone backup. Turn on Settings › Back Up to iCloud Drive to also save a PDF of each document to iCloud Drive › PageWorks.

I deleted a document by mistake.

Open Recently Deleted (in Tools or Settings) and recover it within 30 days.

Can I start a scan with Siri or the Action button?

Yes. Say “Scan with PageWorks”, or add the Scan Document action in the Shortcuts app and assign it to the Action button. Touch and hold the app icon for quick actions.

How do I scan a book?

Choose Book mode and fit the open book in the frame. Each shot is split into two pages, curved pages are flattened, and fingers holding the book are removed. Turn either off for a page from Book Page in the review screen, or for all books in the camera’s ⋯ menu. For Japanese books read right to left, turn on Right-to-Left in the same menu.

How do I hide or erase something on a page?

Open a page and tap Sign. Cover adds a black, white or mosaic box, or covers ID and card numbers automatically. Erase lets you brush over stains or pen marks, which are filled with the surrounding paper color. Everything can be undone or changed later.

How do I add the widget or the Control Center button?

Touch and hold the Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget and choose PageWorks. For Control Center, open it, tap + › Add a Control and choose Scan Document. Both simply open the camera.
